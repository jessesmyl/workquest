📖 Flow Playbook & Guardrails

tap to open

Flow upgrades

  1. Wake up and flow at 9:35 AMStart Push Block 1 within minutes of waking using last night's first-step note. No phone, email, or news first.
  2. Lock in every Push BlockRun a site and app blocker you cannot easily switch off, keep your phone in another room, and keep breaks boring with no scrolling.
  3. Dial tasks to the 4% stretch zoneEach push block should sit just past your current skill level. If bored, add a tighter deadline or higher stakes. If overwhelmed, shrink the scope.
  4. Push through the first 10 to 20 minutesEvery flow cycle begins with a struggle phase. Expect friction at the start of a block before judging whether it is working.
  5. Do active recovery before passive relaxingThe midday walk, cold shower, stretching, and reading are active recovery. Do those before the documentary and frozen fruit.
  6. Run a 20-minute Sunday reviewCheck which blocks hit flow and which dragged, then tighten next week's fill-ins before Monday starts.

Non-negotiable guardrails

Why work ends at midnight, not 1:00 AMThe 90-minute phone-free wind-down plus 8 hours of sleep will not fit if work runs to 1:00 AM. Late nights work great when sleep stays protected. Short nights break the entire flow cycle.
  • Hard stop at 12:00 AM so you hit 90 minutes phone-free and lights out by 1:30 AM.
  • Keep the Pull Block at 1:45 PM so customers, realtors, and crews get fast answers during business hours without breaking the morning or night push blocks.
  • Hold the 9:30 AM wake-up within 30 minutes every day, weekends included, so the schedule runs on autopilot.

Travel mode

  • Anchor 1: same wake-up shifted to local timeShift by about 1 hour per day across time zones and get outdoor light right away.
  • Anchor 2: one Push Block before your phoneProtect 90 minutes for your top task before touching email, messages, or news.
  • Anchor 3: nightly power-downWrite tomorrow's first step, put the phone away 90 minutes before bed, and put on blue-light glasses.
  • Transit: turn travel time into your Pull BlockHandle calls, quotes, email, and admin in airports, flights, and car rides.
  • Training: protect the slot, scale the workoutSwap the 90-minute gym session for a 45-minute hotel or bodyweight workout in the same window.

Travel kit

  • Supplements pre-portioned by day.
  • Blue-light blocker glasses.
  • Sleep mask and earplugs.
  • Site and app blocker already configured on laptop and phone.
